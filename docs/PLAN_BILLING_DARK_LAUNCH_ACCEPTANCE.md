## Delivered

- separate refundable cash and non-refundable included-plan balances, each
  with an independent usage reservation bucket;
- usage reservation/settlement consumes included credit first and posts the
  cash/included/revenue split to the immutable ledger;
- append-only, exact-once UTC calendar-month plan periods;
- atomic monthly fee debit, old-credit expiry, new-credit grant, plan snapshot
  and balanced five-account transaction;
- insufficient-cash and active-reservation rollover failures that write no
  period, fee, credit or ledger transaction;
- concurrency-safe period uniqueness and billing idempotency;
- hourly renewal scheduler, bounded failure audit and `PLAN_PERIOD_DUE`
  monitoring;
- customer and administrator plan changes scheduled for the next period with
  no hidden mid-period proration;
- customer portal cash/plan-credit split, available plan cards, pending change
  notice and immutable period history;
- dynamic `plan_review` launch evidence bound to a canonical SHA-256 of plan
  price, credit, limits and features.

## Activation remains pending

Default plans currently have zero monthly fee and zero included credit. Before
public charging, authorized operators must publish reviewed plan economics,
record both `plan_review` evidence rows, fund a QA tenant through signed Stripe
payment, exercise renewal/refund/non-refundable-credit behavior, and approve
the resulting ledger evidence. No commercial values were fabricated here.
